Project 02项目 02

Physicalization in Practice 物理可视化实践

The Embodiment of Emotions 情感的具身化

This project explores the transformation of intangible human emotions into tangible physical forms. By utilizing natural materials and interactive design, we created an embodiment of feelings that can be seen, heard, and touched, winning the "Student Nominated Winner" and "Excellent Poster" at XJTLU SURF 2025. Currently, the project is continuously being improved and refined, and plans are in place to expand the research scope. 该项目探讨了如何将无形的人类情感转化为有形的物理形式。通过利用自然材料和交互设计，我们创造了一个可以被看见、听到和触摸的情感载体——风铃，并获得了西交利物浦大学 SURF (夏季本科生研究项目) 2025年度“学生提名奖”和“优秀海报奖”。目前，该项目正在持续改进和优化中，并计划扩大研究范围。
